06 Finding new clients
Demo on anonymised data

Lead Hunter — finding companies that match a client’s requirements

Finds companies and businesses that meet given criteria. It gathers companies in a niche and city, opens their websites and unpacks the GTM container — revealing the Google Ads conversion tag, Meta Pixel, call tracking, CRM and online booking that don’t show up in the page’s HTML. It also scans reviews for complaints like “nobody called back”, “long wait” or “they mixed up my booking”.

The system takes its criteria straight from a requirements document. You can find businesses that already pay for ads or new companies with no website, size up a niche in a city or compare several niches. The output is a list of companies with a rationale for each: a report, CSV or Markdown.

Tech stack

Node.js (ESM), zero-dependency pipeline Google Places API (New) Overpass / OpenStreetMap GTM container unpacking Regular expressions instead of ML Gemini on Vertex AI: requirements document → search plan JSON · CSV · Markdown Firebase Hosting
Open the demo Straight to the company table
